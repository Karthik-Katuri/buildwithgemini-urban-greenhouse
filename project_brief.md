# My agent: urban-greenhouse
One-liner: A conversational plant care assistant that helps indoor gardeners keep houseplants thriving with a catalog of plants, care guides, and personalized watering schedules.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Plant care experience level, room lighting conditions, and humidity preferences.
- Tools: Search plant inventory, log plant care events, add custom plants, and query watering schedules.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of house plants with species details, light/water requirements, difficulty, and care tips.
- Image gen: Visual previews of plants in various home environments.
- Sandbox: Calculation of optimal watering frequencies based on pot size, temperature, and season.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: plant diagnosis and watering schedule calculations
